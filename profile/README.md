# Nivalis Nights — Cyberpunk Life & Business Simulation

---

## What Is Nivalis Nights?

Nivalis Nights is a cyberpunk life simulation game developed by ION LANDS and published by 505 Games. Set in a sprawling futuristic city stretching from the ocean toward the clouds, it combines business management, everyday activities, character relationships, and urban exploration.

Players begin with a modest noodle stand and can work toward owning ramen restaurants and nightclubs. Running a business involves managing ingredients, preparing food, and deciding how to spend each day while navigating the city's complex social environment.

Life in Nivalis extends beyond commercial activity. Players can meet residents, develop friendships and rivalries, decorate apartments, grow ingredients, fish from a boat, and take part in activities such as chess tournaments. Conversations and personal decisions shape interactions with the city's diverse inhabitants.

The setting also contains darker elements, including security crackdowns, curfews, and a mysterious serial killer. Balancing business ambitions with personal relationships and urban risks gives the game its distinctive blend of cyberpunk atmosphere and everyday simulation.

## Core Game Features

### Restaurant & Nightclub Management

Build a business from a small noodle stand into a larger operation featuring ramen restaurants and nightclubs. Each venue offers a different setting for managing customers, ingredients, and daily activities.

Business development is closely connected to how players organize their time and resources. Growing ingredients and handling food-related tasks add further layers to the management systems.

### Cooking & Ingredient Growing

Food preparation and ingredient management are important parts of the daily routine. Players can acquire ingredients or grow their own supplies, connecting cultivation with restaurant activities.

These systems encourage players to balance the needs of their businesses with other opportunities around the city. Decisions about resources and daily priorities influence how much time remains for personal activities.

### City Exploration

Nivalis is a vertically layered cyberpunk metropolis filled with distinct districts, residents, businesses, and places to discover. Moving around the city reveals new characters, activities, and opportunities beyond the player's own establishments.

Boat trips offer another way to explore the environment, while the city's atmosphere is shaped by changing weather and the contrast between everyday life and corporate control.

### Apartments & Interior Decoration

Players can buy and decorate apartments, creating personal spaces within the sprawling city. Home decoration adds a creative activity that complements the demands of business management.

The game also allows players to customize living spaces associated with friends, extending decoration beyond their own homes.

### Character Relationships

The city contains a diverse cast of characters with their own stories and personalities. Interactions can develop into friendships, rivalries, or romantic relationships, depending on how players engage with the people they meet.

Social interactions are not simply separate from the rest of the game. Relationships form part of the daily routine, creating reasons to revisit familiar characters and explore different sides of city life.

### Fishing & Leisure Activities

When business responsibilities become demanding, players can spend time fishing or exploring the city by boat. These activities offer a change of pace from managing restaurants and nightclubs.

Chess tournaments and other mini-games add further variety, allowing players to pursue personal interests alongside their business goals.

## City Life & Daily Decisions

### Time Management

Each day presents competing priorities. Players may choose to expand a business, cultivate ingredients, meet residents, decorate an apartment, or spend time exploring.

The balance between work and personal activities is central to the game's life simulation. Choosing one activity means deciding how to allocate time that could have been spent elsewhere.

### Curfews & Urban Risks

Nivalis has a darker side shaped by security crackdowns, curfews, and a mysterious serial killer. Some activities require players to pay attention to their surroundings and the restrictions affecting different areas of the city.

The game also includes rival businesses and opportunities for sabotage, adding conflict to its economic systems. Choices can have consequences, so actions taken against competitors may create new risks.

### Cyberpunk Atmosphere

Neon-lit streets, dense urban districts, corporate influence, and a city extending from waterfront areas toward elevated neighborhoods establish the setting. The contrast between personal ambitions and the larger urban environment is central to the game's identity.

Weather, character encounters, and the range of daily activities help create a setting that combines routine life with a more unsettling futuristic backdrop.

### Mini-Games & Personal Activities

Chess tournaments and other mini-games offer additional activities beyond business operations. These diversions add variety to the daily schedule and create opportunities to interact with residents in different contexts.

Fishing, home decoration, and city exploration also allow players to spend time on personal interests without focusing exclusively on business expansion.
